📖 Stat Definitions
SUBS
Submissions — the number of funding applications submitted to lenders on behalf of merchants. This is the primary conversion activity metric and the most direct indicator of pipeline building.
OFFERS
Funding offers presented to merchants — the number of times an agent receives an approval and presents terms. An offer requires a SUB, but not every SUB generates an offer.
NEW UNITS
Funded deals — merchants who accepted an offer and received funding. This is the ultimate win and the metric that directly generates agent commissions.
TALK MINS/HR
Talk Minutes per Hour — total live call talk time divided by hours worked. Measures phone productivity. A higher number means more time actually speaking to merchants, not just dialing. Pulled from Vaspian/LiveVox reports and calculated per day.
INITIAL APPs
Initial Right Party Contact per Application — the rate at which first outreach attempts reach the actual decision-maker. Measures lead quality and outreach efficiency. Higher = better targeting and less wasted effort. Pulled from LiveVox and Salesforce data.
EXPANSION STATS (unlocked per contest)
Additional metrics (STAT_06 through STAT_15) are reserved for specific contests and competitions. These will be named and defined when a new contest activates them. They do not affect baseline scoring unless explicitly enabled by the commissioner.
📋 Contest Rules & Scoring
Point Values (Current)
SUBS 1 pt per SUB
OFFERS 0.5 pts per Offer
NEW UNITS 2 pts per Unit
TALK MINS/HR 0.1 pts per unit
INITIAL APPs 3 pts per unit
Tiebreakers
When two agents are tied on total points, the winner is determined in this order: (1) SUBS, (2) NEW UNITS, (3) OFFERS, (4) TALK MINS/HR, (5) INITIAL APPs.
W/L/T Record
Head-to-head matchup results are scored: Win = 3 points, Tie = 1 point, Loss = 0 points. Win percentage is calculated as Wins ÷ Total Games Played.
Data Update Schedule
Stats are updated Monday through Friday by 9 AM. Data is pulled from Salesforce, LiveVox, and Vaspian and consolidated by the commissioner. Weekend stats are not tracked. Any questions about a specific day's numbers should be directed to the commissioner.
